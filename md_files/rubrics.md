## 4. Translation (English → Bangla)
 
### Translation — 5 Marks
 
| Criterion | Marks | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy of meaning / faithfulness to source | 2 | The single biggest category — no distortion, addition, or loss of the original meaning |
| Grammar & sentence structure in Bangla | 1 | Correct Bangla grammar and sentence formation, not a word-order copy of the English |
| Vocabulary & word choice | 1 | Natural, contextually appropriate Bangla equivalents — penalize literal/word-for-word translation of idioms or fixed expressions |
| Fluency, naturalness & completeness | 0.5 | Reads like something a Bangla speaker would actually write; nothing omitted or added; tone/register preserved |
| **Extraordinary** (strict) | 0.5 | Idiomatic elegance, deft handling of a culturally-loaded phrase, or a translation that reads better than a literal rendering would |
| **Total** | **5** | |
 

## 5. Basic Paragraph
 
A single-paragraph task is judged on the same bones as the essay (understanding → development → support → structure) minus the counter-argument machinery, since there's no "other option" to dismiss in one paragraph. As marks shrink, categories start merging — same rule as the Creative Writing and Personal Reflection tiers: don't invent precision the mark count can't support.
 
### Basic Paragraph — 15 Marks
 
| Criterion | Marks | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Topic understanding & topic sentence | 1 | Opens with a clear, on-topic controlling idea |
| Development of the main idea | 6 | Main scoring zone — depth, coherence, and focus of the supporting sentences; average students land 3–4 here |
| Examples / specific details & relevance | 2 | Concrete detail, not generic filler; must actually support the point made |
| Unity & logical order | 1 | Stays on the one idea; sentences follow a sensible sequence, no drift |
| Concluding sentence | 1 | Ties back to the topic sentence, doesn't just trail off |
| Flow, language & grammar | 1 | |
| **Extraordinary** (strict) | 3 | Exceptional word choice, an unusually sharp angle, or control well beyond level. Zero for normal students by default |
| **Total** | **15** | |
