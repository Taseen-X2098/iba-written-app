
## 2. Story Completion

Students receive a 30–45-word opening in a separate block, usually ending in
the middle of a sentence. They must copy that opening into their answer and
continue it. The supplied opening is reference material, not a model answer:
any interpretation, genre, plot, or ending is valid when it preserves the
established facts and develops them coherently.

Missing or inaccurate copying must be mentioned in student feedback and judged
inside **Continuity with the opening and prompt adherence**; do not invent a
separate deduction. An unexplained point-of-view or tense switch weakens
continuity or flow, while a deliberate, controlled shift remains valid. An
earned ambiguous ending can receive full credit. A completely unrelated story
must not receive more than 50% of the raw marks.

### Story Completion — 15 Marks

| Criterion | Marks | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Continuity with the opening and prompt adherence | 0.5 | Opening copied and continued directly; preserve established facts |
| Plot development, causality and pacing | 4 | Main scoring zone — original, coherent arc with purposeful complications |
| Characterization, setting and descriptive detail | 2 | Credible characters; specific descriptive or sensory detail |
| Language, vocabulary and narrative style | 2 | Controlled voice, precise vocabulary, sentence variety, purposeful devices |
| Theme, message or emotional impact | 1 | Meaning or emotion grows naturally from events and choices |
| Ending and resolution | 1 | Earned and deliberate; a purposeful open ending is valid |
| Flow, structure, paragraphing and grammar | 1.5 | Consistent viewpoint/tense unless deliberately controlled; strict accuracy |
| **Extraordinary** (strict) | 3 | Exceptional voice, construction, insight, or an unexpected but earned turn |
| **Total** | **15** | |
